As part of its structured recruitment process for the 2026 season, **IEEE Zagazig Student Branch** (IEEE-ZSB) organized a comprehensive Group Discussion phase, serving as the first filtration stage to select potential volunteers. This stage was carefully designed to evaluate candidates in a dynamic and collaborative environment, where participants engage in meaningful discussions, exchange perspectives, and work together to solve problems within a limited timeframe.

The group discussion is a structured and interactive session involving small groups of approximately 8 participants. It is widely used in recruitment, education, and training settings to assess essential soft skills that cannot be fully evaluated through traditional applications. Through this activity, IEEE-ZSB aimed to identify candidates who demonstrate strong communication abilities, critical thinking, teamwork, leadership potential, and the capability to express and present ideas effectively in a cooperative setting rather than a competitive or debate-oriented one.  
![][image1]

**Agenda**

The day began with an engaging **icebreaking session** that lasted for 30 minutes. During this segment, each participant introduced themselves to the group, sharing their name, academic major, and motivation for joining IEEE-ZSB. This activity played a key role in creating a comfortable and welcoming atmosphere, helping participants become more confident and open to interaction before moving into more challenging tasks.

Following the icebreaker, participants moved into the second stage, which consisted of a set of **IQ-based questions** and problem-solving challenges. Working collaboratively under time constraints, each group was required to brainstorm and come up with solutions collectively. This 30-minute segment was designed to test their analytical thinking, creativity, and ability to function effectively as a team under pressure. It also highlighted how participants approach problems, distribute roles, and contribute to group success.  
![][image2]

The final stage of the group discussion focused on **open-topic discussions**. Participants were given a specific topic and asked to discuss it within their groups, exchange viewpoints, and ultimately present their ideas to the entire room. This stage emphasized public speaking, confidence, and the ability to articulate thoughts clearly in front of an audience. It also encouraged respectful listening and constructive dialogue among team members. The duration of this segment was 30 minutes and served as a key indicator of each participant’s communication and presentation skills.

The group discussion phase concluded with formal follow-up communication. All participants received emails informing them of the next steps in the recruitment process. Successful candidates were invited to proceed to the next stage, which involves one-to-one interviews, where their individual competencies and motivations would be further evaluated.

Through this well-structured and engaging process, IEEE-ZSB ensured a fair, inclusive, and effective selection experience, identifying candidates who not only possess strong technical potential but also demonstrate the interpersonal and professional skills required to thrive within the organization
