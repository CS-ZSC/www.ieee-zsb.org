The IEEE PES Eco-Entrepreneurship Competition was initiated in response to a recurring challenge expressed by many students: the difficulty of transforming engineering ideas into real projects due to a lack of financial awareness and feasibility planning. While students often possess strong technical knowledge and innovative ideas, they frequently struggle with estimating project costs, evaluating economic viability, and understanding how to position their ideas within a real-world context. This gap limits their ability to move from concept to implementation, causing many promising ideas to remain undeveloped.  
Recognizing this issue, IEEE ZSB introduced the concept of Eco-Entrepreneurship to bridge the gap between **technical innovation** and **financial feasibility**. The competition was designed not only to encourage students to develop sustainable solutions in power, energy, and environmental sectors, but also to equip them with essential entrepreneurial skills. Participants were guided to think beyond the technical design, considering cost estimation, scalability, and real-world impact as integral parts of their projects.  
![][image1]

Participation in the competition was designed as a structured learning journey, where students first gained foundational knowledge of entrepreneurship through sessions delivered by industry experts. These sessions covered essential concepts such as business modeling, cost estimation, and project feasibility. Following this, participants applied what they learned directly to their own projects, integrating entrepreneurial thinking with their technical designs. Furthermore, all proposed ideas were centered around sustainability and ecosystem impact, ensuring alignment with the mission of the IEEE Power & Energy Society. This approach ensured that students not only understood the theory of entrepreneurship but also practiced it in a real, hands-on context, transforming their ideas into viable, sustainable, and impactful solutions.  
The Competition has a strong impact on global sustainability by empowering students to develop innovative solutions for real environmental and energy challenges. Aligned with the United Nations **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, the event helps prepare a generation of engineers  
capable of driving sustainable change, while contributing to global efforts in clean energy, resource efficiency, and ecosystem protection.

**EVENT Details**

The IEEE PES Eco-Entrepreneurship Competition was successfully conducted as a dynamic **two-day event** (10 days apart), carefully designed to provide both learning and hands-on competitive experiences. The **first day** featured a **business bootcamp** aimed at fostering fundamental entrepreneurship concepts, such as business modeling, cost estimation, and market analysis, delivered by industry experts. This day provided a valuable platform for attendees to connect with peers and engage directly with industry experts. Then the participants were given ten days to work on their ideas, business models, market research with their teams. The **second day** focused on the highly anticipated **Pitch Day**, the core of the event, featuring a distinguished panel of industry professionals, academics, and entrepreneurs in energy and sustainability. Teams presented innovative solutions addressing real-world environmental challenges, aligned with the IEEE PES mission. Their pitches reflected strong preparation, demonstrating clear problem definitions, solid market analysis, and practical, scalable solutions that integrated engineering with entrepreneurial thinking, while being evaluated on technical feasibility, economic viability, and environmental impact.

Upon the conclusion of all team presentations, the judging panel conducted a comprehensive evaluation based on the established criteria and cumulative scores. In recognition of the outstanding quality and diversity of the submitted projects, the top three teams were distinguished across **three specialized award categories: Best Business Model**, **Best Ide*a***, and **Best Influence Project on the Local Communit*y***. Each award was designed to acknowledge excellence in key dimensions of eco-entrepreneurship:

- strategic and financial planning  
- innovation and creativity  
- tangible societal and environmental impact
