The IEEE Zagazig Student Branch (IEEE ZSB) proudly launched its 2026 season with an inspiring **Opening Day** that marked the beginning of a new journey filled with ambition, passion, and teamwork. The event served as a true kick-off, bringing together a new generation of volunteers who arrived full of energy, excitement, and a strong desire to be part of something impactful. 

![][image1]

The day began with an organized registration process, ensuring a smooth and welcoming start for all attendees. This initial step allowed participants to settle in comfortably, get familiar with the environment, and prepare themselves for the experiences ahead.

Following registration, the event transitioned into its first official segment: the opening talks. The session started with an insightful speech from the **Faculty Dean**, followed by an inspiring talk from the branch advisor, **Dr. Mohamed Lotfy**. These talks set the tone for the entire season, offering valuable advice, sharing experiences, and reinforcing the importance of growth, commitment, and continuous learning. Hearing from such respected mentors provided the attendees with both motivation and a clear vision of what lies ahead. 

![][image2]

To break the ice and create a friendly, engaging atmosphere among the new volunteers, an interactive activity was held. Participants were challenged to find missing pieces of a **penguin jigsaw puzzle** scattered across the hall. This fun and dynamic activity encouraged movement, communication, and teamwork, allowing everyone to interact in a relaxed setting while building early connections with one another.

As the day progressed, it was time for one of the most anticipated segments: introducing the new **Executive Committee (EX-COM)** for the 2026 season. Each member shared their personal journey within IEEE ZSB, reflecting on their experiences from previous seasons and how those moments shaped their growth.

**2026 Season EX-COM:**

* Ahmed Raiyah – Chairman  
* Ahmed Medhat – Technical Vice Chairman  
* Mohamed Abdelazez – Managerial Vice Chairman  
* Ahmed Elnaggar – Treasurer  
* Mohamed Emad – Webmaster  
* Engy Mohamed – Secretary

Their stories were not only informative but also deeply motivating, giving the new volunteers a glimpse of the opportunities, challenges, and achievements that await them throughout their IEEE journey.

After this segment, attendees were given a short break to socialize, exchange conversations, and strengthen their newly formed connections in a more informal setting.

The event then welcomed a distinguished guest speaker, **Eng. Saeed Amine**, whose journey in volunteering stood as a powerful example of growth and impact. As a former **Chairman of IEEE ZSB (2017)**, Fundraising Officer Coordinator at IEEE Young Professionals Egypt (2018), and Chairman of NASA Space Apps Cairo (2023), he shared his personal experiences and how volunteering played a transformative role in shaping his career and personal development. His talk was both inspiring and relatable, leaving a strong impression on the newcomers and encouraging them to fully embrace the journey ahead.

To close the day on a meaningful note, a reflective activity was organized where participants shared their thoughts, feelings, and aspirations for the new season. Each attendee wrote their message on a sticky note and placed it on a blank wall, which gradually transformed into a vibrant space filled with dreams, ambitions, and positive energy.

And because IEEE ZSB believes that enjoyment is just as important as dedication and hard work, the day didn’t end there. A **friendly outing** was organized afterward, allowing everyone to unwind, share laughter, and create memorable moments together. The day ended with smiles, stronger bonds, and hearts full of excitement and anticipation for the incredible journey that lies ahead.
